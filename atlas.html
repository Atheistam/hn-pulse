What the data says

  • Front-page stories climb fastest at age 3-6h (median 12.8 pts/h).
  • Decay is a power law, not exponential. Fitting median points/hour against story age gives velocity ∝ age^-1.066 (R²=0.9901, 5 age buckets). An exponent near -1 means a story's scoring rate is roughly inversely proportional to its age — double the age, halve the rate — so cumulative score keeps growing like log(age) instead of flattening on a fixed clock.
  • No hour-of-day claim yet: sampling covers only 4 of 24 UTC hours (20 unsampled), so any apparent "busiest hour" would be an artefact of our own snapshot schedule, not of Hacker News. Published as a known gap rather than a finding.
  • Median front-page turnover: 23.0 new stories per snapshot (median 119 stories on the page).
  • Longest-lived story: GPT-6 Astra — 113.9h on the front page.
