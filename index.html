Points gained = measured increase between our first and last observation of the story. It is a lower bound: stories that spike between two snapshots are invisible.

Method

Plain HTTPS GETs of the HN front page, Show HN, Ask HN and the monthly hiring thread, plus the official Firebase API for exact scores — every ~3 hours since 2026-09-03T20:05:39Z. No auth, no keys. Derived measurements (points/hour, staying power, domain aggregates, front-page turnover) are computed from our own history.

Limitations, stated up front: snapshots land in four UTC windows per day, so hour-of-day claims are sparse and biased; coverage is 6.75 days; short-lived spikes between captures are unmeasurable; the record is survivorship-biased toward stories that stayed on the front page. Full detail in the README.
